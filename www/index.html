Keys

Slide your finger across keys to glide · multi-touch works · MAIN / ENV / OSC / LOFI switch knob banks · drag knobs up/down, or tap − + for fine steps · ▶ and ● under the octave buttons run the beat and arm recording from here · SUS next to them turns on sustain — while it's on, how long you hold a key is how long the recorded note rings for · the AUTO / H1-H4 chips below choose which voice recording targets — AUTO spreads notes across whichever is free (chords work), or pick one voice to record onto it specifically, like arming a track · the small VOL knob next to them is the same level as the SYN fader on the Beats mixer — turn either one and both move · SMP loads your own audio file as the oscillator's waveform — tap it again while active to swap files · 🎙 RECORD SAMPLE records straight from your mic instead (up to 8s) and uses it the same way · LOFI bank: Crackle adds a vinyl noise bed, Wow is tape pitch wobble, Crush is bitcrush grit, Duck pumps the mix on every kick — all start at 0%, turn one up to hear it · CHOP slices the same sample into 7 one-shot pads across the white keys instead of pitching it — black keys are inert, and octave doesn't change anything in this mode.

Beats

Tap drum cells to toggle, drag to paint · Bass cells cycle through notes with each tap · H1-H4 are four independent recorded voices (real four-part harmony) — a note's letter marks where it starts, unlabeled steps in the same color are it sustaining. Tap a note to erase it, tap a sustained step to trim from there on, or drag from a note across empty steps to extend how long it rings · Acc steps make all drums hit harder · the swing chips (STR8 / 54 / 58 / 62 / 66) jump to classic hardware-sampler swing feels · 🎲 MUTATE lightly varies the current drum pattern instead of replacing it · 🎼 GROOVE opens a categorized list of full-patch presets — picking one recalls the drum/bass/harmony pattern and the oscillator and every knob bank, not just the beat · Clear Pattern at the bottom needs a second tap to confirm.

Recording

Press ▶ PLAY, arm ● REC, then play the keys — every note snaps to the grid and lands in whichever H1-H4 voice is free, using your current patch. Play up to 4 notes at once (multi-touch, or a laptop chord) for real harmony — a 5th note is dropped with a toast if all four are busy. Turn on SUS first and a note's length follows how long you actually hold the key, instead of always snapping to one step.

Mixer

Drag faders for volume · tap a channel name to mute · hold it to solo.

Slots

Tap SAVE, then a slot letter to store your pattern · tap a filled slot to load it — switching slots while playing is live arranging.

File

EXPORT / IMPORT save and load your pattern as a small JSON file · EXPORT WAV renders 4 bars of the current beat to a real audio file you can share or drop into any music app.

Laptop

A S D F G H J K play white keys · W E T Y U play black keys · Z / X shift octave · Space starts and stops the beat.
